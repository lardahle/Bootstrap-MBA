High-net-worth individuals investing **their own money** in early-stage companies.

- **Key Points**:
  - Smaller checks and smaller equity stakes than VCs.
  - They invest in **growth potential**, often before revenue.
  - Often the first outside money after friends and family; many also mentor or make introductions.
