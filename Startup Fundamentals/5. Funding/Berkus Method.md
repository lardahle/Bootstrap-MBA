A way to value a **pre-revenue** startup by adding up a value for each risk you've already reduced.

- Classic version (Dave Berkus): up to about **$500k each** for
  1. a sound idea (basic value, product risk),
  2. a prototype (technology risk),
  3. a quality management team (execution risk),
  4. strategic relationships (market risk),
  5. product rollout or sales (production risk).
- The total caps out at about $2–2.5M before revenue. Adjust the per-factor amounts for your market and the year.
