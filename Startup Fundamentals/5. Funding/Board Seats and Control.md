At Series A, VCs usually take **several board seats**.

- **Key Points**:
  - The board can **remove you as CEO**. You'd still own your shares, but you'd no longer run the company.
  - Lecture warning: **don't tie a company role to a board seat.** If you're ever out of commission, you don't want both to go at once.
  - Board seats are negotiated at each round: the balance of founder, investor and independent seats decides who controls the company.
