"Pull yourself up by your bootstraps": fund growth from **revenue or personal money** instead of outside capital.

- **Key Points**:
  - **You keep the equity**, but you also carry the risk.
  - Less money means **slower growth**.
  - A tactic from lecture: sell **peripheral products or services** (consulting, a simpler version, an adjacent service) to:
    - build a customer base and contacts,
    - get your name out there,
    - gain experience in the industry and **learn which problems customers would actually pay to have solved**.
