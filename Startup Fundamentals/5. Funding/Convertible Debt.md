A hybrid: it starts as a loan and converts into equity when a specified event happens, usually the next priced funding round.

- **Key Points**:
  - Often used as a short-term **bridge** between funding rounds.
  - Lets the company and the investor put off agreeing on a valuation until there's more information.
  - The most common form for startups is the convertible note.
