**Short-term debt** that converts into equity at a set future event, such as the next funding round or a milestone.

- **Terms to know**:
  - **Interest rate** (typically 5–10%): the interest also converts into shares.
  - **Maturity date**: if no conversion happens by then, the investor can **call the loan due**. In practice it's usually renegotiated.
  - **Valuation cap**: the highest valuation the note will convert at.
  - **Discount rate** (typically 10–30%): a discount on the price new investors pay in the next round.
  - **"Most Favored Nation" (MFN) clause**: if later investors get better terms, early investors get them too.
- **Example**: $500k raised with a $5M cap and a 20% discount. Series A prices the company at $10M. The cap gives an effective $5M and the discount gives $8M, so the note converts at **$5M**, whichever is better for the investor.
