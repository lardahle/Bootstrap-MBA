Raising small amounts from many people online.

- **Key Points**:
  - **Reward-based** (Kickstarter, Patreon) or donation-based (GoFundMe): you **keep all your equity**.
  - **Equity crowdfunding** also exists: many small investors each get a slice, under specific regulations.
  - Doubles as market validation. Pre-orders show people will pay.
