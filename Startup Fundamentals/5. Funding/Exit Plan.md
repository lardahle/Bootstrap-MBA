Mergers and acquisitions are usually thought of as the **exit**: how founders and investors finally get paid.

- **Key Points**:
  - If you want the idea to reach the world as fast as possible, you can **sell to a larger company and keep a management role**.
  - Payment is often **based on milestones** (an "earn-out"), so part of the price depends on hitting targets after the sale.
  - Investors will ask about your exit early, because it's how they get their return.
