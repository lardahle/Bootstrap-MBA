Early money from your **personal network**, often called "the three Fs."

- **Key Points**:
  - Comes in early, before there's anything to show an angel.
  - The risk is to relationships as well as money: tell them plainly they could lose all of it.
  - Use proper paperwork (often a SAFE or convertible note), so a later investor doesn't find a mess.
