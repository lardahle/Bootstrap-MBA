**Non-dilutive** funding: money you don't repay and that costs no equity.

- **Key Points**:
  - Examples from the course: **NSF I-Corps / I-Site** programs, **SBIR** (Small Business Innovation Research), **STTR** (Small Business Technology Transfer, written "SBTT" in lecture), and local pitch competitions.
  - **Lots of overhead:** applications, reporting and compliance take real time.
  - Well suited to deep tech and research spin-outs, where the timelines scare off investors.
