**Initial Public Offering**: the company's shares start trading publicly.

- **Key Points**:
  - Raises capital for large-scale growth, "going global," and gives early shareholders a way to sell.
  - Run by [[Investment Bankers]]. It's expensive and brings ongoing reporting and compliance duties.
  - It's one exit among several, alongside [[Exit Plan|M&A]] and [[Secondary Sales]].
