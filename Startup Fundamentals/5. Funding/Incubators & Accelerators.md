Programs that provide **community, resources, mentors, contacts and networking**, usually for a small amount of equity or a fee.

- **Key Points**:
  - **Incubator** = early idea stage.
  - **Accelerator** = after you have an MVP or prototype; usually a fixed-length cohort ending in a demo day.
  - Examples from the course: **Capital Factory** (Austin), **Plug and Play** (Bryan–College Station).
