Preferred shareholders get paid **before** common shareholders when there's a **liquidation event** (a sale or wind-down).

- **Participation types** (how much more they get after their preference):
  - **Non-participating**: get paid their preference, then they're done (or they convert to common stock if that's worth more).
  - **Full participating**: get their preference, then **also** share in what's left as if common: "double dipping."
  - **Capped participation**: participate after the preference up to a cap, then convert to common stock.
- For founders, non-participating at 1x is the friendliest. Participation can wipe out what common holders get in a modest exit.
