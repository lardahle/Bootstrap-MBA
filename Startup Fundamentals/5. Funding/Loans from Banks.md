Banks don't lend money to people who need it. They lend to people who have assets and would rather not turn them into cash.

- **Key Points**:
  - Expect to put up **collateral** (property, equipment, receivables, a personal guarantee).
  - Rarely an option for a pre-revenue startup: there's no cash flow to repay from and nothing to secure the loan against.
  - You keep all your equity, but the repayment schedule doesn't care how the business is doing.
