Loans from private individuals rather than institutions.

- **Key Points**:
  - Individuals usually ask for something extra in return: equity, a share of profits, or custom terms.
  - Terms are negotiable, so write them down properly. A handshake loan from a friend can turn into an ownership dispute later.
