Equity financing means selling part of the company. In the professor's words, you are **"giving away your future profits."**

- **Key Points**:
  - You don't repay equity, but every share you sell is a permanent claim on the company's value and, often, a voice in its decisions.
  - Each new round **dilutes** existing owners, founders included.
  - Only sell as much as you need to reach the next milestone that raises your valuation.
