- **Pre-money valuation**: what the company is worth **before** the new investment.
- **Post-money valuation** = pre-money + the new investment.
- **Investor ownership** = investment ÷ post-money.
  - Example: $2M invested at a $8M pre-money valuation gives a $10M post-money valuation, so the investor owns **20%**.
- Convertible instruments set a [[Convertible Notes|valuation cap]] and a discount rate instead of a fixed valuation.
- For pre-revenue startups, see [[Berkus Method]] and [[Scorecard Method]].
