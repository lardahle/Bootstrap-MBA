Firms that **buy, restructure and resell mature companies**.

- **Key Points**:
  - They often target **distressed** companies they can buy at a discount, or spin-offs of underperforming divisions of larger companies.
  - They merge companies, split them up, and use debt to fund the purchase (leveraged buyouts).
  - **Not typically interested in startups.**
