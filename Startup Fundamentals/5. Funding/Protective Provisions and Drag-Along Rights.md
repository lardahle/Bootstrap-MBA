**Protective provisions** are veto rights investors get to protect against dilution and major changes. VC approval is required to:
- sell or liquidate the company (and they get the final say on the terms),
- amend the Certificate of Incorporation or the bylaws,
- raise more capital or take on debt,
- increase the number of shares,
- grow or shrink the Board of Directors.

**Drag-along rights**: under agreed conditions, the VCs can **force the company (and the other shareholders) to sell**.
