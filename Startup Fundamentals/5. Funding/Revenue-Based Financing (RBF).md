Investors are repaid a **percentage of revenue** until a set total is paid back, instead of fixed loan payments.

- **Key Points**:
  - **No equity** is given away.
  - Payments rise and fall with revenue, which is easier on cash flow in slow months than a fixed loan.
  - Only works once you have **steady revenue**, so it's not for pre-revenue companies.
