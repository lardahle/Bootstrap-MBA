The **pre-revenue to early-revenue** stage.

- **Key Points**:
  - **Valuation:** about $2M–$5M (2024 course figures; these move with the market).
  - Usually raised on **convertible instruments**, so the valuation question gets put off: [[Convertible Notes]] and [[SAFEs (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)]].
  - Why convertibles: simple terms, no valuation needed, quick deals.
