The first big **priced** round: "reaching escape velocity."

- **Key Points**:
  - **Valuation:** about $5M–$25M (2024 course figures).
  - **For:** setting up manufacturing, building a sales force, scaling what already works.
  - **Convertible instruments convert** into shares at this round.
  - If you aren't a **C-Corp**, you must convert to one (needed to issue preferred shares).
  - Investors get **preferred shares** (see [[Liquidation Preference]]).
  - A **stock option pool** is created to attract and keep key employees. It comes out of the founders' equity.
  - Expect [[Protective Provisions and Drag-Along Rights]], a [[Right of First Refusal]] and VC [[Board Seats and Control]].
