Series B, C, D and later rounds: money to **grow**.

- **Key Points**:
  - **Series B valuation:** about $15M–$25M and up (2024 course figures).
  - Used for expansion and for **buying complementary businesses**.
  - "The owner of a growing business has no money": growth eats cash, so a successful company still needs more rounds.
  - **Bridge financing** (often convertible) keeps the lights on between rounds without setting a new price too early.
