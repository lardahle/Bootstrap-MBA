Investment arms of large companies.

- **Key Points**:
  - They invest for **strategic** reasons as well as financial return: access to your technology, a partnership, a future acquisition.
  - They can bring benefits money can't: distribution channels, an existing customer base, manufacturing, credibility.
  - Watch for terms that tie you to them and scare off their competitors as customers or acquirers.
