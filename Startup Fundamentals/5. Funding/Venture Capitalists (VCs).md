Firms that invest **pooled money**: they raise funds from other investors (limited partners) as well as their own.

- **Key Points**:
  - They look for companies **about to grow explosively**. A good, steady business is not what they fund.
  - They need a few huge outcomes to make up for many failures, so they push for scale.
  - More hands-on than angels: board seats, protective provisions and a say in strategy (see [[Series A]]).
